# Sound Share privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

Sound Share sends the sound one of your Android devices is playing to another
of your devices on the same local network. This policy explains what the app
does with your information. In short: it collects nothing, and the sound
stays on your network.

## What the app accesses, and why

- **The sound your device is playing**, while you are sharing. Android only
  lets apps capture it after you approve its screen-capture prompt, and that
  capture requires the **microphone permission**. Sound Share never records
  from the microphone and never captures the picture.
- **Your local network**, to find the other device and send it the sound.
- **The device's name** (for example "Ana's tablet"), shown on the other device
  so you can tell which one you are connecting to.

## Where the sound goes

Only to the devices you paired with by scanning or typing this device's
pairing code, over your local network. It is encrypted on the way. It is never
sent to the internet, to us, or to anyone else, and it is never stored.

## What is stored on your device

Your pairing code, the codes of devices you paired with, and your settings,
such as the listening delay. They stay in the app's private storage, are left
out of backups, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting,
and it does not contact any server of ours.

The QR code scanner is provided by Google Play services. Google's handling of
data in Play services is covered by Google's privacy policy.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
